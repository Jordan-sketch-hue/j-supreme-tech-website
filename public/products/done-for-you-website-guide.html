How It Works

The 5-Step Process

From payment to live website in under a week. Every step is transparent — you know exactly what's happening and when.

DAY1

Intake & Brief

Within 4 hours of payment, you receive a brand questionnaire via WhatsApp or email. We collect: business name, services, tagline, target audience, color preferences, and any reference sites you like. This is the only thing we ask of you.

DAY2

Design Preview

We send you a desktop and mobile preview of the homepage design for approval. You can request one round of changes at this stage — layout, colors, copy tone. No code is written until you sign off on the design direction.

DAY3–4

Full Build

We build all 8 sections of your website: Hero, About, Services, Pricing (if applicable), Testimonials, FAQ, Contact, and Footer. WhatsApp CTA integration, Google Analytics, and mobile responsiveness are standard in every build.

DAY5

Staging Review

You receive a live staging URL (e.g., your-business.vercel.app). Review every section. One more round of changes is included here — copy edits, image swaps, layout tweaks. No full redesigns at this stage.

DAY6–7

Launch & Handoff

We connect your custom domain (you purchase, we configure), confirm SSL is active, and push the site live. You receive the full source code via GitHub or ZIP, plus a 15-minute Loom walkthrough of how to make basic edits yourself.

  • Business name and tagline (or we write one for you)
  • List of services you offer and a 1–2 sentence description of each
  • Your target customer (who you serve and where)
