Getting Started

Live in 10 Minutes

1

Start Your 3-Day Free Trial

No credit card required. Visit supreme-suite.vercel.app and click "Start Free Trial." Your account is provisioned immediately via automated email.

2

Configure Your Business Profile

In Settings → Business, add your business name, logo, primary brand color, and contact information. These appear throughout the platform and in all client-facing documents.

3

Connect Your Domain (Enterprise)

In Settings → Domain, add your custom domain. You'll receive a CNAME record to add to your DNS. The platform loads on your domain within 24 hours of DNS propagation.

4

Activate Your First Module

From the left sidebar, activate the first system relevant to your business. Each module has a setup wizard that walks you through initial configuration in under 5 minutes.

5

Invite Your Team

Go to Settings → Team. Add team members by email. Assign roles (Admin, Manager, Staff). Each person logs into their own account with access limited to their role.
