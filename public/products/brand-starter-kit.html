Brand System

Rules That Make Every Touchpoint Consistent

A brand system isn't a logo — it's a set of rules that make every touchpoint look like it came from the same place.

  • Visual hierarchy — which elements get attention first, second, and third
  • Consistency rules — minimum clear space, color substitution on dark vs. light backgrounds
  • Application examples — mockups showing your logo on a website header, business card, and Instagram profile
  • Misuse examples — explicitly showing what NOT to do (stretching, recoloring, adding effects)
