Revenue Milestones Covered

Chapter 7 covers the exact systems needed at each income level — because what gets you to $1K/month will stall you at $3K. Here's the map:

$0 → $1,000/mo

  • One offer, direct outreach, manual delivery
  • No automation needed yet
  • Focus: getting 2–3 paying clients

$1,000 → $5,000/mo

  • Content channel switched on
  • Offer expanded to passive product
  • Simple email list started

$5,000 → $10,000/mo

  • Paid traffic layer added
  • Referral program formalized
  • First hire: VA or part-time contractor

$10,000+/mo

  • Product suite with upsells
  • Team runs delivery
  • Founder works on business, not in it
