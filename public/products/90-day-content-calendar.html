The System

90 Days Broken Into 3 Phases

Not a random mix of post ideas. A structured 90-day strategy with a clear purpose for each phase — building awareness, building trust, then converting followers into buyers.

Phase 1: Awareness

Days 1–30
  • Brand introduction posts
  • Meet-the-team content
  • Behind-the-scenes glimpses
  • "What we do" explainers
  • Community content & local hooks
  • Problem-awareness content

Phase 2: Authority

Days 31–60
  • Tips & educational posts
  • Client testimonials & wins
  • Before/after showcases
  • FAQ answer posts
  • Process & method reveals
  • Industry myth-busting

Phase 3: Conversion

Days 61–90
  • Direct offer posts
  • Limited-time promotions
  • Objection-handling content
  • Social proof stacking
  • Call-to-action sequences
  • Re-engagement campaigns
Content Mix

8 Content Types — One Per Day

Each day is pre-assigned a content type. You never stare at a blank "what do I post today" screen again.

Educational Post

Tips, how-tos, and industry knowledge that position you as the expert. High share rate.

Testimonial / Social Proof

Client wins, reviews, and results. Converts fence-sitters into buyers.

Behind-the-Scenes

Your process, workspace, or team. Humanizes the brand and builds trust.

Product / Service Spotlight

Clear, benefits-focused posts about what you offer. Rotated to avoid feeling salesy.

Community / Culture

Local pride, holidays, relatable moments. Builds connection and emotional resonance.

Engagement Post

Questions, polls, "this or that." Boosts reach by triggering comment activity in the algorithm.

Story / Reel Hook

Prompt-based story content and reel concepts pre-written for each week.

Offer / CTA

Direct sales content — limited slots, specials, or "DM us to get started." Used sparingly, strategically.
